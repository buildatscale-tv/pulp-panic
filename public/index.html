    How to play

    Slice

    Swipe across fruit to cut it. With a mouse, hold the button and drag. On a touch screen, your blade sets how many fingers can cut at once (Spark: 1, Gilded: 5).

    Cut 3 or more fruit in one swipe for a combo. Cut through the exact center for a bullseye. Cut fast, many times in a row, for a blitz.

    Modes

    Arcade: 60 seconds. A bomb costs 10 points. Catch power-ups. At the end, slash the Grand Gourd as many times as you can.

    Classic: no timer. If you miss 3 fruit, you lose. If you cut a bomb, you lose. Each 100 points gives back one miss.

    Zen: 90 seconds with no bombs.

    Arcade power-ups

    Frostberry: time goes slow. The clock goes slow too.

    Blazefig: fruit flies in from the sides.

    Goldenberry: all points are doubled.

    Level up

    Every round gives XP equal to your final score. Each new level gives 1 skill point.

    Spend points in Skills: wider cuts, longer combos, bomb shields, more time, and more. You can reset skills at any time.
